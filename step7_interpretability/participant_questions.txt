INFORMATION FOR PARTICIPANTS (read aloud or shown first)
You will look at short descriptions of banknote measurements and the decisions
made by three computer models that try to tell genuine notes from forged ones.
Your job is to work out what EACH MODEL will say and to read its explanation.
You are not being tested on banknotes, and the model may be wrong: we only want
to know whether you can follow the model. It takes about 15-25 minutes. You can
stop at any time. We record your answers, the time you take, and a participant
ID; no names.

The four measurements (all numbers; higher or lower is not "better"):
  variance   how much the image texture varies
  skewness   how lopsided the texture distribution is
  curtosis   how peaked / heavy-tailed the texture distribution is
  entropy    how random the texture is

Before we start: how familiar are you with machine learning?  none / some / regular

PRACTICE (one per model, with feedback; not scored)
Here is an example of how Model X decided an earlier note, with its explanation card.
[show practice card]  Any questions about the wording?

FOR EACH OF THE 6 CASES  (same wording every time)
  Show the "Inputs:" line only.
  Q1  "What will Model X predict for this note: genuine or forged?"      [start timer A]
      Record the answer, stop timer A.
  Reveal the full explanation card.                                      [start timer B]
  Q2  "Which ONE measurement contributes most strongly to THIS prediction?"
      (variance / skewness / curtosis / entropy)
  Q3  "In one or two sentences: why did this model choose that class?
       Which measurements pushed towards it, and which against?"
  Q4  "How easy was this explanation to understand?  1 (very hard) ... 5 (very easy)"
      Stop timer B.
  Do not comment on whether the answer was right during the real cases.

CLOSING
  "Which model's explanations helped you most, and what was confusing?"
